# 🎬 TransferPath demo plan (2–3 min video)

**Live site:** https://hackathon-two-roan-40.vercel.app

---

## Before you hit record (3 min)

1. Open the live site in **Chrome** and make the window large.
2. Start from a clean state: open DevTools (⌥⌘I) → **Application** → **Local Storage** → right-click the site → **Clear**. Then refresh.
3. **Pre-fill your courses** so the demo is quick:
   1. Go through setup quickly (see Scene 2 for the values).
   2. Open **GE & GPA** and add these courses:

      | College | Course | Grade |
      |---|---|---|
      | Glendale CC | MATH 103 (Calculus I) | A |
      | Glendale CC | MATH 104 (Calculus II) | B |
      | Glendale CC | ENGL 101 | A |
      | Glendale CC | CS/IS 135 | A |
      | Glendale CC | PSYC 101 | B |
      | Pasadena City College | PHYS 1A | In progress |
      | Glendale CC | MATH 105 | Planned |

   3. Check that the GPA tile shows **3.62**.
   4. Go back to the **home page**.
4. Start recording:
   - **QuickTime** → File → New Screen Recording → turn on the microphone, or
   - **Cap** (go.cap.so).

## Scene 1: The problem (≈15 s), Home page
**Say:** "To transfer from a community college to a UC, students open ASSIST once for every college-and-UC pair. Five UCs and ten nearby colleges is fifty tabs, plus admissions dashboards and a GE worksheet on paper. TransferPath puts it all in one place."

**Do:** point at the headline, then click **Continue to my plan** or **Redo setup**.

## Scene 3: My Plan (≈20 s)
**Point at:**
1. The tiles: prep **3/12**, GE areas, GPA **3.62**, units **21/60**.
2. **Take next:** each course with where to take it.
3. **My majors:** progress and chances. UC Davis shows **Guaranteed (TAG)**.
4. **Your courses also fit:** for example Mathematics of Computation.

**Say:** "One screen tells you what to take next, where, and your chances."

## Scene 4: What to Take (≈35 s), the main feature ⭐
**Point at:**
1. The **Where to take your classes** box: **GCC + PCC**. "The fewest colleges that cover every required course."
2. The red line: **Discrete Mathematics (UCB) isn't offered nearby.**
3. The checklist: "Calculus I appears **once**, for all 5 majors, and it's already done."
4. Click **🔀 Compare colleges**, open a major, and scroll sideways. "ASSIST shows one college and one UC at a time. Here it's all of them side by side."

## Scene 5: My Chances (≈20 s)

## Scene 6: GE & GPA (≈25 s)
**Do:**
1. Scroll to the **Cal-GETC checklist**. Point at an area marked ❌ and its rule: "Plain-language rules. No PDF."

3. Scroll to **GPA projection**: "At a 3.5 average on 10 more units, your GPA becomes 3.58. To reach 3.7 you need to average 3.87."
